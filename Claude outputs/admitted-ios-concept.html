Every locked MVP screen in one interactive build: phone auth with real validation, an AI profile builder, a nested bubble home, search, news, a swipe+flip card stack with sourced citations, a timeline & checklist, a true-cost ledger, and profile/settings.

What's in this build

1
Real validation, not just happy-path
Phone/OTP fields reject garbage — disabled CTA until valid, shake + inline error on bad input, a working resend-code cooldown.
2
One list bubble, not four
Safety/Likely/Target/Reach now live inside one "My List" bubble that springs open (iOS-folder pattern) into the four bands plus Timeline and True Cost.
3
Research folded into the AI, not a duplicate bubble
A separate Research bubble would just reopen the same AI chat — so Research lives inside the omnipresent assistant instead, freeing the slot for a real News bubble.
4
No fabricated numbers, anywhere
Every chance/cost figure carries a Verified or Estimated badge and a source. The pre-profile home screen shows a generic popular list with zero personalized stats, on purpose.
5
Timeline, checklist, and true cost, not just the pitch
Deadlines double as a checklist (tap to mark done), and True Cost totals your saved schools over four years — both reachable from the My List bubble.

Tap the numbered steps to jump, or use the phone directly — phone number → 4-digit code → talk to the AI → tap My List → swipe & flip a card.
